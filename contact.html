FAQ

Common questions

What kinds of events do you cover?

Church services, conferences and seminars, concerts and live shows, and other special events. Alpha also offers studio recording and permanent sound installation.

How do I get a quote?

Call +263 776 229 138, message on WhatsApp or use the quote form on this page. Share the event, venue, date and expected crowd.

Do you bring the equipment or only mix?

Alpha sets up PA systems, speakers, mics and mixing desks for events. Tell us what you already have when you ask for a quote and we will plan around it.

Can you test and repair our cables?

Yes. Alpha offers cable testing to find faulty cables before they fail during an event, and cable repair and upkeep.

Which areas do you work in?

Alpha is based in Harare, Zimbabwe. Call to confirm availability for events in other areas.

How early should I book?

As early as you can, especially for large events. Call to check availability for your date.
